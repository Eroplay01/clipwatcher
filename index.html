Automatic clips from your own Twitch live

ClipWatcher is a desktop app that runs on the streamer's own Windows PC while they are live on Twitch. It watches the public chat of the streamer's own channel, detects moments where chat gets excited (or someone types !clip), cuts a 45-second clip of that moment from the recording, adds Portuguese captions, makes a vertical 9:16 version and sends it to the streamer's own social accounts.

How it works

  1. The streamer starts ClipWatcher on their PC and goes live.
  2. ClipWatcher records the stream locally and reads the public chat anonymously.
  3. When chat spikes, a clip is cut and captioned on the PC (nothing is processed on our servers — there are none).
  4. If the streamer connected their accounts, the clip is uploaded:
    • YouTube — as a private Short, so the streamer reviews it before making it public.
    • TikTok — to the streamer's TikTok inbox as a draft. The streamer opens TikTok, edits and posts it themself.

Who it is for

ClipWatcher is a personal tool built for the Twitch channel eroplay01. It only ever posts to accounts that the streamer connected themself, on their own computer.
